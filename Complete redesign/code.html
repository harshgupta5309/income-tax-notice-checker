About Litigation OS

Harsh Gupta // CA Aspirant & System Builder

"I am a Chartered Accountant aspirant and the Income Tax head at my firm. I don't build software because I want to be a tech guru—I build it because the traditional tools of our trade are fundamentally broken, and lives/livelihoods are on the line."

My journey into system design started in the trenches of real tax practice. As the head of Income Tax at a busy firm, I lived the daily nightmare of notice tracking. I watched as critical notices slipped through the cracks simply because the system relies on manual checking.

The Reality of the Trenches

  • The Communication Black Hole: We have clients who don't have active email addresses, or who constantly lose their phone numbers. Getting them to track their own compliance is impossible.
  • The "Too Old for This" Demographic: Many of our clients are elderly, traditional business owners. They are quite literally too old for this—they don't understand the complex online tax portals, and honestly, neither should they have to.
  • The Manual Interval Trap: Checking portals manually at random intervals is a disaster waiting to happen. Missing a statutory deadline under Sec 143(2) or an assessment deadline isn't just an inconvenience; it’s a financial catastrophe for the client and a reputational nightmare for the firm.

Why I Designed Litigation OS

I realized we couldn't rely on chaotic Excel sheets, physical registers, or sticky notes anymore. I needed a digital cockpit that assumed worst-case scenarios:

  • • Notice-First Tracking: Stop grouping by client folders. Tell me which notice is going to blow up first, regardless of who it belongs to.
  • • Double-Verification Safety: Demand absolute scraped proof of portal response status.
  • • No-Nonsense Visibility: A clean interface built for eyes that have been staring at tax compliance screens for 14 hours straight.
"We aren't just filing forms; we are defending our clients' peace of mind. If the system doesn't make that defense foolproof, it isn't doing its job." — Harsh Gupta

System User Manual

How Does It Work?

To check litigation notices, click the Sync Notices button. The automation engine runs the following steps:

  • 1. Launches a secure backend browser session to access the Income Tax E-Portal.
  • 2. Logs in using client credentials from the Excel Registry Sheet.
  • 3. Navigates to the official e-Proceedings panel.
  • 4. Automatically downloads notices in CSV format (AX, BX, AY, and BY files) to the Destination folder.
  • 5. Highlights new notices and lists all downloads for tracking and reconciliation.

Server Delays & Timeout Options

If the portal takes longer than 20 seconds to respond, the system displays a choice box inside the progress window. You can choose to Retry the current client taxpayer from the beginning of login, or Abort the Scraping run entirely.
